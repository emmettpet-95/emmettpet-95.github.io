I study how variability in the upper atmosphere affects satellite drag, orbit decay, and reentry timing.

Research

Atmospheric density and satellite reentry

I compare reentry predictions from WACCM-X, NRLMSIS 2.1, and an exponential atmosphere model against Space-Track TIP decay estimates. My analysis examines how the timing differences vary with geomagnetic activity, season, and prediction lead time.

Selected work

Orbit propagation workflow

I use TLEs and SGP4 to initialize trajectories at a common prediction epoch, then propagate them with different atmospheric density models to compare predicted reentry times.

Reentry statistics

I analyze reentry timing and event rates across geomagnetic conditions, accounting for exposure time in each activity range and comparing a mixed-object sample with a Starlink subset.
